07#1 Question I Cannot Figure Out

Should the projectionhost DLQ and the middleware DLQ be unified into a single dead-letter abstraction?

The tension: There are now two dead-letter stores in the library:

  • middleware.SQLDeadLetterStore / MemoryDeadLetterStore — dispatch-tier, used by RetryConfig.OnDeadLetter, handler-based API
  • projectionhost.DeadLetterStore / MemoryDeadLetterStore — projection-tier, used by the projection host, entry-based API with ReplayDeadLetters

ADR-0043 decided "keep them separate (option C)" because dispatch and projection lifecycles are genuinely different. But every time I look at this, I see two stores with 80% overlapping fields (ProjectionName/HandlerName, EventType, Error, Event, Reason) and a consumer who will eventually ask "why do I have two DLQ tables?"

Why I can't resolve this: Unifying them risks conflating two genuinely different retry semantics (per-message dispatch retry vs. per-projection-position replay). Keeping them separate risks the "split brain" anti-pattern the brutal self-review flagged. This is a domain-modeling decision that depends on how consumers actually use both tiers in practice — and we don't have enough real consumers yet to know. It needs a deliberate architectural decision, not an autonomous one.
