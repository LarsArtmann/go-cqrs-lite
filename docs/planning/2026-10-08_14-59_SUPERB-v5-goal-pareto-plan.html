The honest verdict (readiness checklist, 2026-10-01)

The v5 cut is NOT blocked on code volume. Phases 1–7 of ADR-0123 are done; the v007 removal census holds every deletion bidirectionally; Layer-2 pre-cut migrations are already in-tree. The cut is blocked on 3 Layer-0 owner rulings (ADR-0139 encryption 4Qs · sweep §4(a) SQL renames · ADR-0146/0147 lease+direction), 1 quiet-window MySQL leg (Layer 1), and the stalled v4.x publish waves that must land before the branch point. Everything else is mechanics with existing gate coverage.

Why this order (the Pareto logic)

W0 (1% → 51%): one ruling session + one publish train unblock Layers 9–11, the freeze surface, and consumer trust. W1 (4% → 64%): three quiet-window runs convert every "gated on quiet window" row into receipts and satisfy Layer 1. W2 (20% → 80%): additive master-safe work (module extraction, cursors, AsyncAPI, engine-surface completions) that must precede the freeze. W3–W5: the branch, deletion cascade L4→L8, the big builds (fold, encryption, FilterOp), docs, the cut. W6: closure evidence + parallel backlog.
